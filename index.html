About

I am a PhD student based in the Department of Geosciences at the University of Oslo.

Research interests

  • Using satellite observations to investigate how aerosols acting as ice-nucleating particles (INPs) influence cloud phase.
  • Seeking observational evidence for the role of marine organic aerosols as INPs.
  • Developing retrieval algorithms for aerosol and cloud properties to study aerosol–cloud interactions (ACI).

  • Ph.D. University of Oslo2025–Present (2028? We’ll see)
