About

I am a PhD student based in the Department of Geosciences at the University of Oslo.

Research interests

  1. Using satellite observations to investigate how aerosols acting as ice-nucleating particles (INPs) influence cloud phase.
  2. Seeking observational evidence for the role of marine organic aerosols as INPs.
  3. Developing retrieval algorithms for aerosol and cloud property to study aerosol–cloud interactions (ACI).
